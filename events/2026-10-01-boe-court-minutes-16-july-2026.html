Minutes of the Meeting of the Court of Directors held on 16 July 2026.

Sarah Breeden, Deputy Governor – Financial Stability

Clare Lombardelli, Deputy Governor - Monetary Policy

Sir Dave Ramsden, Deputy Governor – Markets & Banking

Sarah John, Chief Operating Officer

Sebastian Walsh, Secretary of the Bank

Katharine Braddick, Deputy Governor – Prudential Regulation

1. Conflicts, Minutes of 4 June 2026; Matters Arising and Twelve-month Forward Planner

There were no conflicts declared in relation to the present agenda.

The minutes of the meeting held on 4 June 2026 were approved.

2. Governor’s Update

The Governor updated Court on recent developments, including the impact on energy prices due to the renewed tensions in the Middle East.

The Governor highlighted the recent Financial Stability Review last week, which noted a number of key risks to financial stability, including the ongoing evolution of AI and its application. The Governor said the Bank was supporting banks to ensure they could gain sufficient access to AI models to protect their cyber security. The Governor noted that the FPC also agreed a package of proposed changes to the capital framework for banks.

3. Audit and Risk Committee (ARCo) Update

Jonathan Bewes gave an update on the recent meeting of ARCo.

In the audit discussion, ARCo had discussed the external auditor’s performance and reviewed EY’s management letter following the conclusion of the audit. Internal Audit presented its annual report and updated ARCo on recent audits.

In the risk discussion, ARCo had received the quarterly risk report and the mid-year update on the balance sheet and capital projections. ARCo was also updated on testing of material controls across the organisation.

ARCo reviewed the Finance Modernisation Programme before its subsequent go live and received an update on a recent critical business resilience exercise with outside authorities.

4. Remuneration Committee (RemCo) Update

Diana Noble gave an update on the recent meeting of RemCo. RemCo was updated on the Pay Framework Review.

Court asked that final outcomes from the Pay Framework Review come to Court.

5. Nominations Committee (NomCo) Update

The Chair updated Court on the recent meeting of NomCo. NomCo had discussed succession planning. NomCo also discussed the mentoring and talent development options for staff.

6. Frontier AI impact on the Investment Portfolio

(Afua Kyei, Jo Hill and Zoe Hawtin-Rew)

Zoe Hawtin-Rew introduced the paper, outlining that Court approval was being sought for additional investment to address challenges posed by frontier AI in the current financial year. The Governor noted that this work was vital.

Court approved the additional funding.

7. Finance Update

Afua Kyei updated Court on the Bank’s finances.

Afua noted that the Bank was on track to hit its cost saving target this year.

Governors noted progress on the Mutually Agree Resignation (MAR) scheme exits. Non-Executive Directors noted the importance of controlling the backfill rates from the MAR scheme to avoid having to make further cost cuts later. Governors said that there was a robust process in place to challenge proposals for new external recruitment, with most vacancies only open to internal applicants, which prevented cost increases at the Bankwide level.

8. Strategic Planning - Business Planning 26/27 Update - assumptions and cost challenge

Jo Hill updated Court on business planning and noted the plans to pilot a move to a continuous delivery model for investment programmes. Directors discussed the cost challenge for 27/28. Non-Executive Directors raised that the targets should be reviewed annually as circumstances change. Directors agreed on the importance of improving business processes to make the Bank more efficient and asked that this return to Court as part of the business planning item.

Directors noted the opportunity to add developing process improvements into managers’ objectives and to identify ways for some of the benefits of process improvement to be felt directly by staff to incentivise innovation in this area.

9. COO Update

(Sarah John, Jane Cathrall and Vijay Mistry)

Sarah John updated Court on the initial results from the Viewpoint survey. Directors agreed on the importance of delivering the programmes already underway.

10. Future backing-asset arrangements for banknotes

Tom Smith introduced the proposal for the future backing-asset arrangements for banknotes and noted that the proposals were contingent on MPC decisions relating to the stock of gilts in the Asset Purchase Facility held for monetary policy purposes.

11. Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) internal evaluation of Financial Market Infrastructure (FMI)

(Melissa Davey, Louise Eggett and Sasha Mills)

The IEO introduced the evaluation and said that it had found that FMID had implemented the changes from the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023 effectively, and that the FMI Committee was effective. The evaluation did, however, make a number of recommendations for the Bank to consider.

Sasha Mills said that the Bank should use the publication of the evaluation and the management response to explain the Bank’s approach, especially with regard to points raised by industry, and that a number of the recommendations are already in progress.

Directors discussed the recommendations in the evaluation and the importance of considering industry feedback. Melissa Davey said that the IEO would note in the publication the progress made during the evaluation.

Court welcomed the IEO report and management response and asked that they be published in due course.

Court delegated agreement of a foreword and executive summary to the Governor and Chair of Court. Court asked that the final report to be circulated to Court ahead of publication.

12. The Bank’s Legal Risk Appetite

(Rob Price and George Johnston)

George Johnston presented the updated articulation of the Bank’s legal risk appetite and said that the intention was to support pragmatic, considered, risk taking in decision making. Court heard that the process for legal advice would not change, with Legal Directorate (LD) continuing to review all papers coming to decision making committees.

13. Chair’s Effectiveness Review of Financial Policy Committee (FPC)

The Chair presented his effectiveness review of the FPC, noting the committee was operating effectively.

14. Committee Appointments and Conflicts

Court noted the paper and discussed future recruitment.

Court delegated to Anne Glover the appointment of a new Bank appointed member of the pension scheme trustees.

Anne Glover, the Senior Independent Director, presented her annual review of Court effectiveness, noting Court was well chaired and functioned as an effective unitary Board.

15. Papers for Information

- Monetary Policy Committee Report

- Annual Report and Accounts Signing Committee minutes

- The Bank’s Senior Managers’ Regime Statement of Responsibilities and Responsibilities Map

- Approved minutes from Committee meetings since the last meeting of Court on 4 June 2026

- ARCo minutes 22 May 2026

- RemCo minutes 30 April 2026

The meeting of Court was closed.
